YOUR ATTENTION
IS YOURS.

But how much of it are you actually choosing to give away?

THREE QUESTIONS

STOP FOR A SECOND.

You do not need to delete every app. The first step can simply be noticing what happens when you use them.

Notice the moments when your hand reaches for your phone automatically: waiting, boredom, silence or habit.
Pay attention to when your original reason for opening an app has disappeared but the scrolling continues.
The goal is not perfect digital behaviour. It is becoming more intentional about where your attention goes.

BREAK THE SCROLL

DO SOMETHING
DIFFERENT.

The next time you catch yourself endlessly scrolling, try replacing a few minutes of screen time with something else.

01

GO OUTSIDE

Take a short walk, get some fresh air, or simply spend a few minutes away from your screen.

02

MAKE SOMETHING

Draw, write, cook, take photographs, play an instrument, or work on a creative project.

03

CONNECT WITH SOMEONE

Call a friend, have a conversation, or spend time with someone without checking your phone.

04

MOVE YOUR BODY

Stretch, exercise, dance, or try a physical activity that gets you moving.

05

LEARN SOMETHING

Read a book, practice a skill, explore a topic, or watch an educational video with a purpose.

06

DO NOTHING FOR A MOMENT

Let yourself be bored. Sit quietly, look around, and give your mind a break from constant stimulation.
